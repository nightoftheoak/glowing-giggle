Temperatures are the official daily high and low recorded by the National Weather Service at Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (KDFW), the official Dallas–Fort Worth climate record, retrieved from NOAA through the Applied Climate Information System (ACIS). Plano has no official station of its own, and none of the stations nearer Plano has a complete record back to 1980; using one station throughout keeps long-term trends free of jumps from switching instruments. DFW is about 20 miles south-west of Plano, and its overnight lows run a few degrees warmer than north Collin County's. On any day DFW didn't report, Dallas Love Field (KDAL) is used and labelled. The daily average used for warm-up and cool-down dates is NOAA's standard (high + low) ÷ 2. The last 7 days are “provisional” because stations sometimes correct their reports; after that, values are never changed. The current reading and today's range so far come from DFW's hourly observations (api.weather.gov), so they can miss a brief peak that the official daily high later captures. “Normal” is the average for that calendar date over the period chosen in “Compare against”, smoothed over ±3 days.
