Daily highs and lows are from the Open-Meteo historical archive (ERA5 reanalysis, ~9 km grid centred on Plano), recorded daily by this repository's GitHub Action. Rows marked “provisional” are recent estimates that are replaced once the archive publishes final values (about five days later); after that, values are never changed. The current reading is fetched live from Open-Meteo. “Normal” means the average for that calendar date over the 20 years before the current year, smoothed over ±3 days.
