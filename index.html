Temperatures are the official daily high and low recorded by the National Weather Service station at McKinney National Airport (KTKI), about 13 miles north-east of central Plano, retrieved from NOAA through the Applied Climate Information System (ACIS). Plano has no official station of its own. On the few days KTKI didn't report, Dallas Love Field (KDAL) values are used and labelled. The daily average used for warm-up and cool-down dates is NOAA's standard (high + low) ÷ 2. The last 7 days are “provisional” because stations sometimes correct their reports; after that, values are never changed. The current reading and today's range so far come from KTKI's hourly observations (api.weather.gov), so they can miss a brief peak that the official daily high later captures. “Normal” means the average for that calendar date over the 20 years before the current year, smoothed over ±3 days.
